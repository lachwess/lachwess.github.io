Ripstick



I first learned how to ripstick around 2016, by clinging to the walls in the hallway of my best friend's house. I would ride it regularly until September of 2019, when it was destroyed along with most of my childhood home by water damage from hurricane Dorian. In 2023, I found one at a thrift store and took to it like a duck to water. Since then, I've obtained a few different ones, with my favorite being a Peacemaker themed one that my roommate painted for me as a Christmas gift. When I'm struggling with artist's block, I'll take a ride around to think. It's meditative.
