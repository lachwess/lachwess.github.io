 Strawberry Mansion (2021)

Strawberry Mansion by Albert Birney and Kentucker Audley is a movie very dear to my heart. Its combination of dreamcore aesthetics, anti-corporate message, old technology, and low-budget special effects make it not only a movie that I enjoy, but one that connects with me and my artistic practice on a deep level. I would talk about it more but I would hate to spoil this beautiful movie for anyone. Go watch it. Patreon Watch
