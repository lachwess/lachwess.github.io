    • Strawberry Mansion (2021)



    Strawberry Mansion by Albert Birney and Kentucker Audley is a movie very dear to my heart. Its combination of dreamcore aesthetics, anti-corporate message, old technology, and low-budget special effects make it not only a movie that I enjoy, but one that connects with me and my artistic practice on a deep level. I would talk about it more but I would hate to spoil this beautiful movie for anyone. Go watch it.

    It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia season 16 episode 8: "Dennis Takes a Mental Health Day"



    While Always Sunny played a very large part in shaping my sense of humor, one episode in particular stands out as being relevant to my work. Throughout the episode, Dennis encounters obstacle after obstacle, all in the form of technology that's supposed to make life easier. At the end of the episode, Dennis tracks down the CEO of a car company at his beach house and pulls his sill-beating heart out of his chest and eats it. This episode manages to successfully outline many of my frustrations with modern technology into a little over 20 minutes and was what initially got me thinking about the enshittification of the internet as a potential topic for my BFA.
